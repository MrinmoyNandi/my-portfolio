# Inbox

Drop markdown files about yourself here: bio, projects, skills, links, anything.
They don't need any particular format. Then ask your AI assistant:

> Update the portfolio from the inbox.

It will follow `../AI_UPDATE_GUIDE.md`. It maps your notes into `content/`, validates them, builds the site,
and moves the processed notes to `inbox/processed/`.

Put images next to your notes or in `public/media/`, and mention in your notes what each image is for.
